The Pub

On quiet, residential Grove Lane

The Kerfield Arms is a corner pub on Grove Lane, just off Camberwell Church Street. The building dates to 1863. For a time it was called The Crooked Well; it has been restored under its original name.

It opened in April 2025 under Adam Symonds and Rob Tecwyn, the team behind The Baring in Islington. Head chef and co-owner Jay Styler was previously head chef at The Baring.

The cooking is simple and considered, built on seasonal produce and humble ingredients, and cooked over fire.
